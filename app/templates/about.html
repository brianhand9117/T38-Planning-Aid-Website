About T-38 Planning Aid

Mission

The T-38 Planning Aid is a comprehensive flight planning tool designed to support T-38 Talon operations across the United States. Our mission is to provide pilots, planners, and operations specialists with current, accurate airport data to enhance flight safety and operational effectiveness.

What We Do

We aggregate and curate data from multiple authoritative sources including:

  • FAA Airport Data (NASR)
  • Runway and facility specifications
  • Government fuel contract databases
  • Community pilot feedback and comments
  • Historical operations data

Features

Our KML files provide:

  • Color-Coded Pins: Quick visual identification of airport categories
  • Runway Details: Length, surface type, and landing distance available
  • Fuel Availability: Government contract fuel locations
  • JASU Locations: Air start cart availability for emergency starts
  • Community Feedback: Pilot comments and operational notes
  • ForeFlight Integration: Seamless import for flight planning

Data Accuracy

Disclaimer: While we strive for accuracy, all users are responsible for verifying information independently. Current Airport Facilities Directories (A/FD) and IFR Supplements remain the authoritative sources. Use this tool as a planning aid only.

Community Driven

We welcome feedback, corrections, and updates from the pilot and operations community. Your inputs help keep this data current and relevant. Contact us with comments or corrections.

Technical Details

Update Frequency: Monthly (1st of the month)\
Format: KML 2.2 (Google Earth, ForeFlight compatible)
Data Sources: FAA NASR database, government contracts, community submissions
Supported Platforms: ForeFlight, Google Earth, WinPlan, and other KML-compatible software
